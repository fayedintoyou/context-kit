# Triage policy

An urgent request (an outage, lost data or a security problem) is triaged within
the hour, at any time. Every other request is triaged on the next working day.

When the team needs more from the customer (steps to reproduce, an account name,
a screenshot), the request waits on the customer and the clock pauses until they
reply.
